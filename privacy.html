Scale Local privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy covers the computer-based Scale Local application, optional Google Health sharing, and this website.

Data and purpose

Scale Local stores scale measurements, measurement times, device information, and sharing settings/status on your computer to provide local history and optional sharing. The maintainer does not automatically receive your measurements or Google authorization data.

Google connection

Sharing is optional and requires your authorization. Google account identity and locally stored authorization credentials maintain your chosen connection. Google's write permission also covers other health measurements and changes to app-added data; Scale Local only uploads eligible new weights and measurement times, including time-zone information. It does not read your Google health history.

Google processes shared records under its privacy policy. The application does not sell data or use it for advertising, developer analytics, or AI training.

Scale Local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Protection

Stored data and authorization credentials use operating-system access controls. Requests to Google use HTTPS. You are responsible for protecting your computer and backups.

Retention and your choices

Local data stays until you remove it. To delete it, stop the app and remove its stored data and any exports or backups. Authorization credentials can be removed by disconnecting.

You can pause sharing, disconnect, or revoke access in Google Account connections. This does not delete records already sent to Google; manage those through Google Health's data controls.

Website

This website does not receive health data or Google credentials and has no analytics. GitHub Pages may process standard web-request information under the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the public issue tracker. Do not post health data or credentials.
