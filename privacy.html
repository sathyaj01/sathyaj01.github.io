Scale Local privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Scale Local is a personal-use app that processes measurements, times, device information and sharing settings for weight history. Google account identity and authorization credentials are used only to connect your chosen account.

With your consent and sharing enabled, only eligible new weights and measurement times are uploaded to Google Health. Google's write permission is broader than weight, but this app does not read health history.

Records are stored locally or, if you enable Azure storage, uploaded with device identifiers and raw scale data to your configured Azure Cosmos DB account. Credentials remain on your computer with operating-system access controls; cloud transfers use HTTPS. The app does not send records to the maintainer, sell data, or use it for ads or AI training.

Local history stays until you delete it; Azure-mode queued measurements are removed after confirmed saves. To delete local data, stop the app and remove its stored data, exports and backups. You can disconnect or revoke Google access; this does not delete cloud records. Azure and Google records remain until you delete them using those services' data controls. Azure processing is subject to the Microsoft privacy statement.

Scale Local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

GitHub Pages may process website-request data under its privacy statement. Privacy questions: public issue tracker (do not post health data or credentials).
